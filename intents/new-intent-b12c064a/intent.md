# Business Intent: Product Financial Analysis - Last 2 Months

## Overview
Analyze the financial performance of all products sold during the last 2 months (December 2025 - January 2026) to understand revenue trends, product profitability, and sales performance across different product categories.

## Business Questions
1. **What is the total revenue generated by each product in the last 2 months?**
2. **Which product families (Cameras, Controllers, IoT Devices, Services, Accessories) contributed the most revenue?**
3. **What are the sales volumes and average selling prices for each product?**
4. **How do discounts impact revenue across different products?**
5. **What is the month-over-month revenue trend (December vs January)?**
6. **Which products are the top performers vs underperformers?**

## Data Sources
**Salesforce Sales Cloud** (`salesforce_sales_cloud.db`)
- **OpportunityLineItem**: Product line items with quantity, pricing, discounts, and service dates
- **Product2**: Product master data including product names, codes, and families
- **Opportunity**: Opportunity details including close dates, stages, and win status

## Key Metrics
- **Total Revenue**: Sum of TotalPrice from OpportunityLineItem
- **Gross Revenue**: Revenue before discounts
- **Net Revenue**: Revenue after discounts
- **Sales Volume**: Total quantity sold per product
- **Average Unit Price**: Mean UnitPrice per product
- **Average Discount %**: Mean discount percentage per product
- **Revenue by Product Family**: Aggregated revenue by product category
- **Month-over-Month Growth**: Revenue change between December 2025 and January 2026

## Time Period
- **Analysis Window**: December 1, 2025 - January 31, 2026
- **Date Logic**: Use `ServiceDate` from OpportunityLineItem when available; fallback to `CloseDate` from Opportunity for won opportunities (IsWon = 1)

## Expected Outputs
1. **Product Revenue Report**: Detailed revenue breakdown by product with quantities, pricing, and discounts
2. **Product Family Performance**: Aggregated financial metrics by product family
3. **Monthly Trend Analysis**: Month-over-month comparison of revenue and volume
4. **Top/Bottom Performers**: Ranked list of products by revenue contribution

## Success Criteria
- Accurate revenue calculations reflecting both gross and net (after discount) amounts
- Clear visibility into product-level and category-level performance
- Actionable insights on which products drive the most value
- Identification of pricing and discount patterns across product families
